# Code of Conduct

life-os-scaffold is a small personal-productivity project. The collaboration
surface is modest — issues, pull requests, and the occasional discussion —
but the expectations for how we treat each other are not.

## Our Pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that helps build a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, skill levels, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code and ideas.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any contributor.
- Publishing others' private information — such as a physical or email
  address — without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: the GitHub repository,
issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other channel
where someone is representing the project or its community.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in
public spaces — for example, posting from an official account or acting as
an appointed representative at an event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the
maintainer:

> [Open a private security advisory](https://github.com/felipefelixarias/life-os-scaffold/security/advisories/new)

Yes, the same channel as [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) — it is the only
private, maintainer-visible channel this repository exposes. Title your
advisory "Code of Conduct report" so it is easy to triage, and include:

- A description of the incident.
- Links to the relevant issue, PR, or comment.
- Anything else you think is important.

If GitHub's private advisory flow is unavailable for your account, open a
minimal public issue titled "Code of Conduct — please contact me" without
details, and the maintainer will arrange a private follow-up.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and safety of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
Responses to reported incidents may include, depending on severity and
context:

1. **Private warning** — a direct message explaining what was wrong and
   what is expected going forward.
2. **Public correction** — a public note on the relevant comment or PR,
   with the offending content edited or removed.
3. **Temporary ban** — a time-limited ban from interacting in the
   repository.
4. **Permanent ban** — for severe or repeated violations.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project
owner.
